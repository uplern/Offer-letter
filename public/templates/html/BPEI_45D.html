Roles & Responsibilities

During the internship and training period, your responsibilities may include:

  • Identifying, researching, and mapping potential corporate, institutional, and educational partners for strategic collaborations.
  • Conducting professional outreach, B2B email campaigns, LinkedIn networking, and partner engagement.
  • Assisting in preparing impactful partnership proposals, collaboration pitch decks, and meeting agendas.
  • Coordinating and participating in partnership exploration meetings, discussions, and follow-up calls.
  • Managing partner relationships, communication channels, pipeline tracking, and active follow-ups.
  • Supporting market research, competitor benchmarking, and new business opportunity identification.
  • Maintaining accurate CRM records, partnership tracking sheets, and weekly performance reports.
  • Collaborating cross-functionally with recruitment and marketing teams to align partnership deliverables.
  • Participating actively in required meetings, training sessions, performance reviews, and team activities.
